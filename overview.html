Menu Card Catalogue

A standalone, installable tool for viewing the middleware Menu Card as a Product Catalogue.

Import the Menu Card Excel workbook and the app turns it into a clean, searchable catalogue of every middleware service — with its effort and cost figures read straight from the workbook. It runs entirely in your browser: no server, no sign-in, nothing uploaded. Spun out of the Middleware Delivery Portal so the "just let me see the catalogue" job stays simple.

Import in a click

Drop or choose the Menu Card .xlsx. It's parsed on your device in a moment — no upload.

Search & filter

Component and size pills, a search box across every column, and per-column filters.

Faithful figures

PD, Story Points, TCS / FTE / Total cost shown exactly as the approved Menu Card has them.

Save as PDF

Export the whole catalogue to a PDF — download on desktop, share sheet on mobile.

Installable PWA

Install it on your laptop or phone; it works offline after the first load.

Mobile-friendly

On a phone the table becomes neat stacked cards. Light and dark themes included.
